Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short

In One Sketch makes short educational videos. Our app is a private tool that publishes those videos to our own accounts. It collects as little as possible, keeps it on our own computer, and never sells or shares it.

What the app receives

When one of our accounts is connected, the platform shares the following with the app:

  • Basic profile information: the account ID, display name, username and profile picture. We use these only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens: keys that let the app upload videos to that account, within the permissions that were approved.

The app does not read your messages, followers, contacts, viewing history or any other content.

How we use it

  • To upload and schedule In One Sketch videos to the connected account.
  • To show the connected account in the app, so the right video goes to the right channel.

Videos are only published after we approve them ourselves.

Where it is stored

Everything stays on our own computer. Access tokens are stored encrypted and are only sent to the platform they belong to. We do not use a server or database in the cloud for this data.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share this data with anyone. It is only exchanged with the platform it comes from (for example YouTube or TikTok) to make the upload work.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google security settings.

TikTok

TikTok's handling of data is covered by the TikTok Privacy Policy. You can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep the data only while the account is connected. When an account is disconnected, its tokens and profile information are deleted from the app and the access is revoked with the platform.

This website

This website has no accounts, forms, cookies or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; both may log basic technical data such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not meant for anyone other than In One Sketch and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Open an issue on GitHub or send a message to @inonesketch.
