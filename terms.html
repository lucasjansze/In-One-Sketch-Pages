Who we are

In One Sketch is an educational channel that makes short videos explaining one concept at a time, such as how the internet, computers and algorithms work. We publish these videos on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

The In One Sketch app

The In One Sketch app is a private publishing tool. We use it only to upload and schedule our own videos to our own channels. It is not offered to the public, and there is no way to sign up for it.

When an In One Sketch account on a platform is connected to the app, the account owner signs in with that platform and approves the requested permissions on the platform's own consent screen. The app only uses those permissions to publish our videos, to read the statistics of our own videos and to show which account is connected.

Disconnecting

A connected account can be disconnected at any time, in the app or in the security settings of the platform. After disconnecting, the app can no longer access the account.
